# Engineering Placements Prediction

Publisher: Tejashvi (`tejashvi14`). [Kaggle source](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tejashvi14/engineering-placements-prediction). Downloaded version 6 directly from the public Kaggle API; publisher metadata lists **CC0: Public Domain**. `source.json` preserves the version, source URLs and SHA-256. `collegePlace.csv` is the unmodified downloaded file.

The publisher describes university engineering-placement records from 2013 and 2014. These are imported public labeled records, not generated by JobJugaad. Original collection and university identity have not been independently audited; no contemporary/BPUT representativeness is claimed.

2,966 rows, 8 columns; PlacedOrNot=1 for 1,639 records and 0 for 1,327. Source fields: Age, Gender, Stream, Internships, CGPA, Hostel, HistoryOfBacklogs, PlacedOrNot. CGPA means through semester 6; HistoryOfBacklogs means ever having a backlog, not current active backlog count. Stream covers Civil, Computer Science, Electrical, Electronics And Communication, Information Technology and Mechanical. CGPA is recorded as integers 5–9 and internships as counts 0–3.

The model uses CGPA, Stream, Internships and HistoryOfBacklogs only. Age, gender and hostel residence are excluded by a predeclared design choice, not empirical feature selection; this does not establish fairness. There are only 181 distinct four-field profiles (58 have mixed outcomes), and 1,022 distinct seven-field profiles. Keep identical modeled inputs together in training/test splits. Do not mistake 2,966 rows for 2,966 independently distinguishable profiles. Do not manufacture skills, projects, resumes or offers for these records or insert them as named tenant accounts.
